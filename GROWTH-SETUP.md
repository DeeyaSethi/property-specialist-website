# Growth setup: the two biggest free wins (do these once)

These two are the highest-impact free things, and only you/your father can do them
(they need his Google login). Budget ~30–45 min each. Do Search Console first.

---

## A. Google Search Console — get the pages found on Google

This is what gets the inner pages indexed and shows you what people search to reach the site.

1. Go to **search.google.com/search-console** and sign in with the Google account
   that should own the site data (ideally your father's business Google account).
2. **Add property → "URL prefix"** and enter `https://propertyspecialist.in`.
3. **Verify ownership.** Easiest options:
   - **DNS TXT record** (most reliable) — Google gives you a `google-site-verification=...`
     value; add it as a TXT record where the domain is registered. *Or*
   - **HTML tag** — Google gives a `<meta name="google-site-verification" ...>` tag.
     Send it to me and I'll add it to the site in 2 minutes, then you click Verify.
4. Once verified: **Sitemaps** (left menu) → enter `sitemap.xml` → **Submit**.
5. **URL Inspection** (top search bar) → paste each of these, then click
   **Request indexing** for each:
   - `https://propertyspecialist.in/`
   - `https://propertyspecialist.in/plots-in-sonipat`
   - `https://propertyspecialist.in/plots-in-gurgaon`
   - `https://propertyspecialist.in/farmland-near-vrindavan`
   - `https://propertyspecialist.in/listings`
6. Check back in a week under **Pages** — the inner pages should move to "Indexed."
7. **Bonus (2 min):** repeat at **bing.com/webmasters** — you can import straight from
   Search Console. Bing also feeds ChatGPT search.

## B. Google Business Profile — the biggest free lead source

This puts him on **Google Maps** and gives free Call / Directions / WhatsApp buttons
for searches like "property dealer near me" and "plots in [city]".

1. Go to **business.google.com**, sign in with your father's Google account, and
   **create or claim "Property Specialist."**
2. **Primary category: "Real estate agency."** Add secondaries that fit:
   *Real estate consultant, Property developer, Land surveyor.*
   Tip: open Google Maps, find the top plot dealer in his main city, copy their category.
3. **Address / service area:**
   - If he's happy to show the office address, use it (it's the strongest ranking signal).
   - If not, set it as a **service-area business** (hide the address, list the towns).
   - Add the areas he genuinely covers (Vrindavan, Sonipat, Gurgaon and nearby).
   - Reality check: **one profile ranks on the map mainly near its address** — you can't
     rank the map pack in three distant cities from one pin. The other two cities we rank
     through the website city pages we built. Do **not** create fake profiles for cities
     with no office — that gets the account suspended.
4. **Verification** is usually by **video** in India: film the signage, the area/landmarks,
   and business documents. Don't change the name/address/category mid-verification.
5. **Fill everything:** phone `9910070102`, website `propertyspecialist.in`, hours,
   services (residential plots, farmland, farmhouse land), a short description, and a
   **WhatsApp link** as the appointment/website action
   (`https://wa.me/919910070102`).
6. **Photos** — upload real land / site-visit / drone photos, and keep adding them.
   Land sells on visuals; profiles with photos get far more calls.
7. **Posts** — once a week, post a new plot or a "site visits this Sunday" update.
8. **Reviews** (huge for both trust and Maps ranking):
   - In the dashboard, get your **review short-link** (Reviews → "Get more reviews").
   - Turn it into a **QR code** on a site-visit card, and send it on **WhatsApp right after
     a good site visit.** Ask every happy buyer. Aim for 25+ at 4.5★ and above.
9. **Q&A** — post and answer the questions buyers actually ask: clear title? loan available?
   approved (DTCP/MVDA/RERA)? price per gaj/acre?
